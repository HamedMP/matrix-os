01 · Color

Living color, grounded by teal.

Teal anchors the identity. Coral, Gold, Green, and Blue create rhythm and communicate state. Give every screen one dominant color relationship instead of using the whole palette at once.

02 · Type

Expressive when speaking. Quiet when working.

Bricolage Grotesque gives the brand a distinct voice. Geist keeps product UI crisp. Geist Mono separates machine output from human language.

Build what matters.

Matrix OS brings tools, conversations, and autonomous work into one calm environment. Hierarchy should be obvious before color or decoration is added.

03 · System

Minimal is a behavior.

A slim interface is not merely sparse. It prioritizes one action, responds quickly, and removes every element that does not improve comprehension or control.

One clear next step

Reserve primary styling for the action that advances the user.

Structure before decoration

Use spacing, type, and grouping before adding borders or color.

Platform-aware parity

Share tokens and behavior while respecting native shell conventions.

Calm motion

Use short transitions to explain state changes, never to delay them.

Accessible by default

Keyboard, focus, contrast, zoom, and reduced motion are design inputs.

Real states

Design loading, empty, error, reconnecting, and success alongside the ideal path.

04 · Access

Color earns its place through contrast.

Use Ink or Teal for long-form text. Supporting colors are best as surfaces, indicators, and large accents unless their pairing has been tested at the rendered size.
